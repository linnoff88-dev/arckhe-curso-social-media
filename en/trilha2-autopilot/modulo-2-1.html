This is the video's system. One brand voice file + one adaptive prompt, and the assistant interviews you (three questions), researches what's working on your platform today, writes the post in your voice — and schedules it through your connected posting tool. You just approve.

Before you start (~10 minutes, once)

Coding assistant installed and signed in → claude.com/claude-code (the assistant’s web app works too—just send your brand voice file in the chat)

Your brand voice file—grab the template that comes with this pack (brand-voice-template.md), fill it out, or let Prompt 1 build it for you

Optional — auto-posting: a posting MCP like Blotato connected to your coding assistant (blotato.com → API key → their MCP guide → connect your social accounts). No MCP? The assistant gives you posts ready to copy and paste. Ask your coding assistant: "list my connected MCP tools"
