BUILD YOUR OWN SPARK The starter package: seven copy-and-paste prompts that turn your code assistant into your own AI social media employee. It learns how YOU write, drafts your posts, proposes content for the whole week, waits for your approval on each one, and even tries automatic DMs for the first time. You won't touch a single line of code.

What you’re about to build Twenty minutes, five prompts, an employee who never sleeps.

On the last page, you’ll have a social media employee running on YOUR computer: an office with a warm cream background at localhost:4780, where SPARK drafts posts that sound like you (not AI), creates a full week of content on demand, and stacks each draft on an approval desk, where nothing moves forward without your yes.

Honesty first: this is the initial build. My finished SPARK took seventeen versions to get right. This package builds the draft office, the foundation everything else rests on. Actual post publishing, the AI carousel studio, and the comment machine came later, and every limitation you encounter in the initial build is a finished feature waiting in the full version. The bonus page near the end gives you a first taste of the automated DM path, and the last page tells you where the finished version is.

You need exactly three things:

1 Coding Assistant The AI that builds and runs everything. Free at claude.com/claude-code. If you can open it and type, you’re qualified.

2 Google Chrome For the SPARK office. Any modern browser works; Chrome behaves better.

3 An API key for the assistant’s brain From console.anthropic.com, with a few dollars of credit. It’s the only key the beginner needs. The assistant will show you exactly where to click.

The only rule: you never type commands or touch files. Every block in this package is a message you paste INTO the coding assistant. The assistant handles the technical work. That’s the whole method.
