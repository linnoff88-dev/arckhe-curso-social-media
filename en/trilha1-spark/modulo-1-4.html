SPARK — Social Media Employee · Module 4/6

Step 3

Give my SPARK an approve desk. Every draft he writes now lands as a card in a column on the right: the platform, the draft text, and three buttons. APPROVE copies the text to my clipboard and marks the card done, so I can paste it straight into Instagram or LinkedIn. REDO IT sends it back with my note and he rewrites it. REJECT kills it, and he asks one short question about why, so he learns my taste. Cards save to disk and survive restarts. Nothing ever gets posted anywhere automatically. I am the publisher, he is the drafter.

Why clipboard instead of auto-posting: actually publishing requires platform connections and safety mechanisms, which is part of the finished build. The starter pack workflow is honest: it drafts, you approve, you paste. That already saves you the hardest hour of the day.
