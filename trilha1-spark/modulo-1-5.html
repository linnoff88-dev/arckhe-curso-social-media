SPARK — Funcionário Social · Módulo 5/6

Etapa 4

Teach my SPARK to pitch me the week. Add an ember-orange button called PITCH ME THE WEEK. When I press it, he drafts 6 posts for my niche in one go: 3 educational, 1 promotional, 1 behind the scenes, and 1 engagement question. Each one lands as its own card on the approve desk with a one-line note on WHY he thinks it will work. Typing "pitch me the week" in chat should do the same thing.
